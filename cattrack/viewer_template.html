How far a held posture may drift per minute and still read as sleep. A collar rotates, so the absolute angle means nothing — but drift still does, and it clusters about 9× more than chance even with movement held fixed. Van Hees’ 5° is far too loose here: it passes 90% of still epochs. The default sits near where temperature agreement peaks.
Stretches shorter than this are scored as still-but-awake. Van Hees uses 30 min, but that targets one consolidated nightly sleep window in humans; cats nap polyphasically, so 5 min is a judgement call of mine, not a published figure.
